• Translated physical network behavior and commercial objectives into a large-scale, nonconvex mixed-integer nonlinear programming (MINLP) model. Decisions included procurement volumes, sales allocation, node pressures, pipeline flows and directions, compressor operating modes and pressure ratios.

• Modeled flow conservation, pressure bounds, supply and demand limits and tiered purchase and sales prices. Incorporated elevation-adjusted Weymouth equations and discrete transmission-direction choices to represent high-elevation pipelines and bidirectional flows.

• Applied nonlinear correction with discrete decisions fixed to update gas compressibility and pressure-ratio bounds. Developed repair strategies for infeasibility caused by linearization and numerical errors, and tested and visualized solution results.

• Delivered planning outputs for gas purchasing, transmission routes and quantities, compressor pressure ratios and sales allocation. Extended modeling to multi-period planning and pipeline linepack management.

• On national-network test scenarios, the custom SLP approach achieved more than 10× speedups over Gurobi 11 nonconvex MINLP / piecewise-linearization baselines in multiple cases. Associated research produced two journal papers and four patent publications.
