# Meta HR 英语面试复习汇总

**岗位：** Manufacturing Operations Engineer  
**团队 / 产品：** Meta Reality Labs / Smart Glasses（智能眼镜代工制造）  
**面试阶段：** HR Screening  
**复习原则：** 简单英语、真实经历、自然表达、突出岗位匹配度。

## 一、核心面试定位

希望 HR 对我形成的印象：

> An operations research professional who can quickly understand complex manufacturing processes, identify production bottlenecks, and develop practical solutions to improve manufacturing operations.

中文：能够快速理解复杂制造流程、识别生产瓶颈，并设计实际解决方案来改善制造运营的运筹优化专业人士。

四个关键词：
- Manufacturing Planning（制造计划）
- Problem Solving（问题解决）
- Data-driven Decisions（数据驱动决策）
- Cross-functional Collaboration（跨团队合作）

## 二、六大核心问题与英文回答

### Q1. Tell me about yourself.（请介绍一下自己）

**建议时长：约 80 秒**

Hi, I'm Jinfeng. Nice to meet you, and thank you for your time today.

I have around eight years of experience in operations research and optimization, with a strong focus on manufacturing planning and scheduling.

Currently, I work at HSBC as an Operations Research Scientist, where I focus on workforce planning and resource allocation.

Before joining HSBC, I worked at Youhualin for more than four years. I led a small algorithm team and worked on several manufacturing projects.

One of my key projects was for Panasonic Electronic Materials. I worked closely with their production planning team to improve capacity planning and production scheduling.

In this project, we developed a planning solution to manage production orders, machine capacity, mold resources, and material availability. The solution helped reduce production costs and improve on-time delivery.

I'm very interested in this opportunity at Meta, especially because the role focuses on smart glasses manufacturing.

I believe my experience in manufacturing planning, problem-solving, and working with production teams could help improve manufacturing operations.

Thank you.

**记忆提纲：** 8 年运筹经验 → 汇丰 → 悠桦林 → 松下项目 → Meta 智能眼镜岗位。

### Q2. Why do you want to leave HSBC?（为什么考虑离开汇丰？）

**建议时长：约 40 秒**

HSBC has been a good experience for me, and I've learned a lot about workforce planning and operations.

But I've found that my current role involves a lot of data preparation and internal coordination, with fewer opportunities to solve complex optimization problems.

What I really enjoy is solving challenging business problems and seeing real improvements from my work.

That's why I'm interested in moving back to manufacturing, where I have strong experience.

I think this opportunity at Meta is a good match because I can use my experience in production planning and optimization to help improve manufacturing operations.

**记忆提纲：** 认可 HSBC 的收获 → 工作重心不匹配 → 更喜欢制造业 → Meta 是合理的下一步。

**注意：** 不要抱怨数据权限、流程保守或同事；不能给 HR 留下不喜欢基础数据工作和跨部门协调的印象。可以补充：

> I understand that data preparation and coordination are important parts of the job. But I'm most motivated when I can use that information to make decisions and improve operations.

若 HR 问 *Are you saying that you don't enjoy your current job?*，可回答：

Not exactly. I think every job has routine tasks, and I understand they are necessary.

I've gained valuable experience at HSBC, especially in working with different teams and understanding business processes.

But I've realized that I'm more interested in manufacturing operations, where I can work on production challenges and see the impact of my solutions more directly.

So it's more about finding a role that better matches my interests and long-term career goals.

### Q3. Can you tell me about your Panasonic project?（请介绍一下松下项目）

**建议时长：约 75 秒**

Sure. This was a manufacturing planning and scheduling project for Panasonic Electronic Materials.

The main goal was to improve production efficiency and on-time delivery.

One of the biggest challenges was that production involved several steps, and different products needed different machines and molds.

We also needed to consider material availability, machine capacity, and delivery deadlines.

My role was to lead the algorithm team, work with the production planning team to understand their needs, and design the planning solution.

We built a system that could create production plans based on available resources and business priorities.

After the system was introduced, mold changes were reduced by around ten per month, saving approximately 300,000 RMB in monthly cleaning costs.

The on-time delivery rate for confirmed delivery dates also reached 99.9%.

This project taught me how important it is to understand real factory operations and work closely with production teams.

**记忆提纲：** 项目目标 → 复杂约束 → 个人职责 → 解决方案 → 30 万元/月成本节约、99.9% 确认交期达成率。

### Q4. You don't have direct CM experience. Why are you a good fit?（没有代工厂管理经验，为什么适合？）

**建议时长：约 65 秒**

Although I don't have direct experience managing contract manufacturers, I believe my background is highly relevant to this role.

One of my biggest strengths is my ability to quickly understand different manufacturing processes and identify key production problems.

I've worked on manufacturing projects in different industries, so I'm used to learning new processes, understanding production constraints, and finding bottlenecks.

I'm also good at turning complex manufacturing problems into clear mathematical models and using data to find better solutions.

For example, in my Panasonic project, I worked closely with the production planning team to improve capacity planning and scheduling.

Besides technical skills, I have experience leading a small team and working with different business teams to make sure our solutions can be used in real operations.

I know there will be new things to learn, especially in smart glasses assembly, testing, and CM management.

But I believe my problem-solving skills, manufacturing experience, and ability to learn quickly will help me adapt to this role.

**记忆提纲：** 承认经验差距 → 快速理解新制造场景 → 瓶颈识别与建模 → 松下证明 → 愿意学习 CM 业务。

### Q5. Why are you interested in Meta?（为什么想加入 Meta？）

**建议时长：约 35 秒**

I'm interested in Meta because it works on innovative products, especially smart glasses.

I think smart glasses manufacturing involves many interesting challenges, such as capacity planning, production efficiency, quality, and working with manufacturing partners.

These are closely related to my previous manufacturing experience.

I also like the opportunity to work with different teams and solve problems at a larger scale.

I believe this role would allow me to use my existing skills while continuing to learn and grow.

**记忆提纲：** 智能眼镜产品 → 生产运营挑战 → 自己的相关经验 → 未来成长。

### Q6. How would you learn smart glasses manufacturing?（如何快速熟悉智能眼镜制造？）

**建议时长：约 50 秒**

I would start by understanding the full production process, from material preparation to assembly and testing.

I would spend time at the factory, talk with production engineers and operators, and learn how each process works.

Then I would look at key production data, such as output, cycle time, machine capacity, yield, and downtime.

My goal would be to identify the main bottlenecks and understand what is causing them.

After that, I would work with the manufacturing team to evaluate possible improvements, such as better production planning, resource allocation, or process changes.

I would start with small improvements, measure the results, and then look for larger opportunities.

This is similar to how I approached manufacturing projects in the past.

**记忆提纲：** 学习流程 → 深入工厂 → 分析指标 → 找瓶颈 → 评估方案 → 小范围验证与推广。

## 三、松下项目：必须记住的事实与数字

| 指标 | 数值 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | 7,000–10,000 | 六个月规划范围内的订单量 |
| Materials | 10,000+ | 物料种类 |
| Mold changes | 约减少 10 次/月 | 系统上线后减少的换模次数 |
| Estimated cleaning-cost savings | RMB 300,000/月 | 估计每月节约的清洗成本 |
| Confirmed-date delivery fulfillment | 99.9% | **确认交期**达成率，不等于全部订单交付率 |

三句补充表达：

> We had to balance machine capacity, mold availability, material supply, and delivery deadlines.

我们需要平衡设备产能、模具可用性、物料供应和交付期限。

> Instead of looking at each production step separately, we considered the whole production process.

我们不是孤立地考虑每个生产工序，而是考虑整个生产流程。

> The goal was not just to build a good model, but to find a solution that the factory could actually use.

目标不仅是建立好的模型，还要找到工厂真正能够使用的解决方案。
